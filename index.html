Japan Job Search

Japan Job Search is a small private tool that helps one person find entry-level job postings and keep track of applications.

It reads public job listings, summarizes how well each one fits, and places the results in a Google Sheet and Google Docs that the tool itself creates. It does not apply to jobs, send email, or post anything on anyone's behalf.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
